Visual & Interaction Style:
- Aesthetic: Ultra-realistic, metallic, premium energy drink visual identity inspired by Red Bull and the RF-Bull cans.
- Color palette: Brushed aluminum / chrome silver, deep metallic royal blue, vibrant metallic magenta/pink, and subtle dark carbon/slate contrasts.
- Metallic & light reflection effects: Realistic gradients, sheen/gloss hover highlights mimicking condensation and cold aluminum cans.
- Interactive elements:
  * 3D card tilt / floating effect on hover for the cans and product cards.
  * Interactive flavor selector / showcase where clicking a flavor changes the highlight, background glow, and taste notes.
  * Dynamic image gallery / carousel displaying the lifestyle, fresh fruit, and motorsport scenes.
  * Smooth animations, high energy badge pills, and pulse/glow CTAs.
